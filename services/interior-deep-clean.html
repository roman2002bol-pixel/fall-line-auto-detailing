What's Included

A cabin that looks and smells brand new

If your exterior is in decent shape but the inside needs real attention — spilled drinks, pet hair, kids, commuting life — this package is built for you. We focus entirely on the interior, going deeper than a standard vacuum and wipe-down.

  • Seat and carpet shampooing
  • Steam cleaning for deep-set dirt and odors
  • Dashboard and trim polishing
  • Interior glass, vents, and door jambs
Base price starts at $220. Final price is confirmed on-site based on your vehicle's size and condition — SUVs and trucks typically run 20–40% above the base price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can this remove pet hair and odors?

Yes — steam cleaning and shampooing are specifically effective on embedded pet hair and lingering odors. Let us know the specifics when booking.

Does this include the exterior?

No, this package is interior-only. If you want both, our Full Detail package covers interior and exterior together at a combined price.

How long does it take?

Typically 90 minutes to 2.5 hours depending on vehicle size and interior condition.
